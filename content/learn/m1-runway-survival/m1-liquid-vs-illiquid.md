Not all money is the same kind of useful. **Liquid** money is spendable now — checking, savings,
cash — with no delay and no cost to access it. **Illiquid** money is real but locked up: home
equity, a 401(k) before retirement age, a car. Selling or accessing it takes time, incurs fees or
penalties, or both.

Runway only counts liquid money, on purpose. A household with $400,000 in home equity and $200 in
checking has real net worth but almost no runway — that equity can't pay this month's rent without
a home-equity loan, a sale, or another slow, costly process. Net worth answers "what am I worth."
Runway answers "what can I actually reach if I need it in the next 30 days," and those are
different questions with different correct answers.

Your current liquid balance is **{{liquid.formatted}}**. Separately, your savings rate — the share
of income you're keeping rather than spending — is **{{savings_rate.formatted}}**. Savings rate is
what feeds liquid cash over time; it's the input, and liquid is the running balance.

:::worked
Someone contributes 15% of every paycheck to a 401(k) and keeps 5% in a savings account. Their
overall savings rate might be 20%, but only the 5% strengthens their runway this year — the 401(k)
portion is real saving, but it's illiquid until retirement and doesn't help a near-term cash
shortfall.
:::

:::faded
Look at your own accounts. Name one thing you own that counts toward net worth but would take more
than a week to turn into spendable cash: ______________. Why it's slow or costly to access:
______________.
:::

:::check
**Question:** Why doesn't home equity count toward runway?
- A. Home equity isn't real money
- B. It can't be spent without a slow or costly process — a sale, a loan, a refinance
- C. Runway ignores all assets except cash accounts by an arbitrary rule
**Correct answer:** B — runway measures what you can access on short notice, and equity requires a slow, often costly conversion step first.
:::

:::apply
**Apply — Create a goal.** Set a liquid-cash goal separate from any net-worth or investing goal you
may already have, using {{liquid.formatted}} as the starting point.
:::

:::
